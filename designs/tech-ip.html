FAQ

Common questions

Should I register my trademark in Cyprus or the EU?
It depends on where you trade. A Cyprus mark covers Cyprus only, while an EU trademark covers all EU member states. I can recommend the right route for your business.
Does the GDPR apply to my small business?
If you process personal data of people in the EU — customers, employees or newsletter subscribers — the GDPR generally applies, whatever the size of the business.
Who owns code written by a freelancer?
Without a written agreement, ownership may not pass to you automatically. A clear contract should assign the rights to your company.
