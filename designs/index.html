About Polykarpou Law

Polykarpou Law is an independent law practice in Limassol, founded by advocate Sofia Polykarpou, a member of the Cyprus Bar Association. Sofia earned her Bachelor of Laws (LLB) at Nottingham Trent University and went on to complete a Master’s in International Business Law at the University of Liverpool, giving her a strong foundation in corporate and cross-border commercial matters.

The practice focuses on corporate and commercial law, advising on company formation and registration, corporate governance and compliance, shareholder agreements, commercial contracts, and the legal side of business transactions and restructuring. Clients include local entrepreneurs, international companies establishing a presence in Cyprus, and foreign investors, reflecting Limassol’s role as a centre for international business.

At Polykarpou Law, every client works directly with Sofia. You can expect clear advice in plain language, honest guidance on your options and costs, and prompt replies to your questions. Services are available in Greek, English and Russian.

Whether you are starting a new company or need ongoing legal support for an established business, you are welcome to get in touch to arrange an initial consultation.
