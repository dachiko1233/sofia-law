Overview

A smooth move to Cyprus.

Every year many families, professionals and investors relocate to Limassol. The right permit depends on your nationality, your plans and your income — and a well-prepared application saves months.

FAQ

Common questions

Which permit is right for me?
That depends on your nationality, whether you will work in Cyprus, and your income or investment. I review your situation and recommend the most suitable route. [Confirm current schemes before publishing]
Can my family come with me?
In many cases spouses and children can apply to join you. The conditions depend on the type of permit you hold.
How long does an application take?
Processing times vary by permit type and the authorities’ workload. A complete, well-prepared file is the best way to avoid delays.
