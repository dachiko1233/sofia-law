FAQ

Common questions

Do I have to answer police questions without a lawyer?
You have the right to consult a lawyer. It is usually wise to speak with one before giving a statement.
What happens at a remand hearing?
The court decides whether you can be held in custody while the investigation continues. A lawyer can challenge the request and argue for your release.
Can you help a foreign national arrested in Cyprus?
Yes. I can advise in English, Greek or Russian and coordinate with family or consular contacts where needed.
